# Delete your FellowHaven account

You can delete your church's FellowHaven account anytime, right in the app.

## In the app

1. Open FellowHaven and sign in.
2. Tap the gear at the top of Home to open **Settings**.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
4. Enter your password to confirm.

That's it. Your account is deleted right away.

## Or ask us by email

If you can't get into the app, email **office@theospreparation.com** from the address you signed up with, and write "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in account (email and password)
- Your church's profile and contact info
- Your list of hidden churches
- Every need your church posted, with all the responses to them

## What stays

- Responses you sent to other churches' needs stay with those churches, no longer linked to an account. Ask us by email if you'd like those removed too.
- A daily post counter (a date, a count and a need ID, nothing personal), so deleting can't be used to get around the posting limit.
- Reports, which aren't linked to anyone.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://fellowhaven.com/privacy) for details.
