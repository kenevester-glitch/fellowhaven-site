Welcome to FellowHaven

Churches were never meant to stand alone. FellowHaven connects the churches of your city so they can share what they have and help carry what others need, whether that's chairs for an outreach, volunteers for a food drive, or a van for youth camp.

How it works

  1. Post a need

    Tell other churches what you need, like chairs, volunteers, a musician or a van, and by when.

  2. Other churches respond

    Churches nearby see your need on the Needs Board and send a short response offering to help.

  3. You connect directly

    You get each other's contact info and work out the details together, by email or phone.

Post a need, offer a hand, and watch the churches of your city work together as one family. It's free, and it's built for churches.
