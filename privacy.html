Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

FellowHaven is a free app that helps local churches in your city share what they need and help each other. It's made by Theos Preparation LLC in Cincinnati, Ohio ("we" or "us"). This policy explains, in plain English, what information FellowHaven collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We collect only what the needs board needs to work: your sign-in email, your church's profile, and the needs, responses and reports people send.
  • No ads. We never sell your information. No tracking or analytics.
  • You can delete your account anytime in the app: Settings > Delete account.
  • You can report a post, or hide a church's posts, anytime.

What we collect

Your account. Churches sign in with an email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your password; Firebase stores it in a protected (hashed) form. Like most online services, Firebase also records technical details such as IP addresses to keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse.

Your church profile. When your church posts its first need, you add your church's name, city and state, and a contact email, a phone number or both. You choose which contact details to share, and you can edit them anytime in the app.

Needs. What your church posts: a title, a short summary, details, a category, an optional "needed by" date, and your church's name, city and state.

Responses. When someone responds to a need, they send their name, their church's name and a short message. If they're signed in, the response is also linked to their account, so they can find it again under "My responses." You don't need an account to respond; if you're not signed in, you also give a phone number or email so the church can reach you.

Reports. Anyone can report a need that looks wrong. A report holds only which need it's about, the reason, and the time. It doesn't say who sent it.

Churches you hide. If you choose "Hide posts from this church," we save that church's ID and name so its posts stay hidden for you. Signed in, this list is stored privately with your account, so only you can see it; signed out, it's kept on your phone only.

A daily post counter. To limit spam, we count how many needs each account posts per day (the date, a count and the ID of the latest need).

Settings on your phone. The app saves a few settings on your device only, such as your light or dark theme choice, whether you've seen the welcome screen, and which responses you've already read. These never leave your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Why we collect it

Only to run the needs board and connect churches with each other: to let churches post needs, let others respond, show each side how to reach the other, keep out spam, and look into reported posts. We don't use your information for anything else.

Who sees what

  • Needs are public. Anyone using the app can see posted needs, along with the posting church's name, city and state.
  • Your church's contact info. The email and phone your church chooses to share are shown to anyone who responds to one of your needs, and to churches you respond to while signed in. Please treat them as public, and share only contact details you're comfortable with, like your church office's.
  • Responses are private. A response is seen only by the church that posted the need and by the person who sent it (if they were signed in).
  • Reports are seen only by us, to decide whether a post should be hidden.
  • Us. We can see the information stored in the service to run it, fix problems and handle reports. We look only when we need to.

We don't share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on Google's servers in the United States. Google protects it with encryption and secure connections. You can read Google's own privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

No ads, no selling, no tracking

FellowHaven has no ads. We don't sell, rent or trade your information. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and our website doesn't either.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account (also under Account) and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in account,
  • your church's profile and contact info,
  • your list of hidden churches,
  • every need your church posted, along with all the responses to them.

A few things stay:

  • Responses you sent to other churches' needs stay with those churches, because they're part of those churches' conversations. They're no longer linked to an account you can sign in to. If you'd like them removed too, email us.
  • The daily post counter (a date, a count and a need ID, nothing personal) stays, so deleting an account can't be used to get around the posting limit.
  • Reports stay, because they aren't linked to anyone.

If you can't use the app, email office@theospreparation.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days. More at https://fellowhaven.com/delete-account.

Children

FellowHaven is for churches and the adults who serve them. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version at https://fellowhaven.com/privacy with a new effective date. If the change is important, we'll also mention it in the app.
